Actuarial science (UP curriculum)

Four companion courses sequenced from time-value-of-money foundations through single-life actuarial machinery and general-insurance risk modelling to advanced life contingencies. See the financial mathematics, actuarial mathematics, risk modelling, and contingencies landings for the full sequences.

  • builtFinancial mathematics (7 lectures, all built): cashflows, accumulation and force of interest, the rate-conversion grid, inflation, level and increasing annuities, equations of value and project appraisal
  • builtActuarial mathematics (6 lectures, all built): survival models, life tables, annuities, assurances, variable benefits, premiums and reserves
  • builtRisk modelling (8 lectures, all built): loss distributions, individual and collective models, reinsurance, ruin theory, Bayesian credibility
  • builtContingencies (8 lectures, all built): multi-state, multi-decrement, joint-life, pensions, unit-linked, profit testing, reserves
