Life insurance, ASSA F102

Internal environment

The insurer itself. Five overview lectures establish what a life office is and how its numbers work, then five modules go deep: what it sells, how it prices, how it manages risk, how it values what it owes, and how it invests. Fifty-seven of the track's sixty-four lectures sit here.

How this module is organised

Read the five overview lectures in order first. They are the frame: a life insurer promises money contingent on human life, collects premiums up front, pays claims decades later, and must hold capital in the gap. Everything in the sub-modules is a consequence of that shape.

After the overview, the sub-modules can be read in any order, but the intended sequence is products, then pricing, then risk management, then risk measurement, then investment. Products supplies the vocabulary; pricing supplies the models; risk measurement reuses both. Investment sits last because it is the only module that starts from the liabilities and works outwards to the assets.

Overview, five lectures

  • built01. Business model — a life insurer is a borrower before it is an underwriter. Premiums arrive at a mean time of 9.5000 years and the benefit leaves at 20.0000, a gap of 10.5000 years or 0.5250 of the term, and that gap is the business: the illustrative endowment collects 20000.0000 against a 30000.0000 promise, a cash loss of 10000.0000, but accumulates 34719.2518 at five per cent for a profit of 4719.2518, with a break-even rate of 0.037189 and identical underwriting on both sides of it. That is the exact reverse of banking, where money goes out first. The summary’s income statement lists two costs that are payments to nobody, the increases in reserves and in solvency capital, so growth reduces reported profit: a static book reports 602.5000 per policy in force and one growing at 0.4000 reports 378.2466, a fall of 0.3722 on identical experience, which puts management’s two listed short-term aims in direct conflict. Free assets are a geared residual at 0.1000 of the balance sheet, so a 0.0500 basis strengthening costs 0.4000 of them, a gearing of 8.0000 that equals the ratio of reserves to free assets exactly, and the summary’s note that the SCR’s placement is country specific matters because moving it takes the gearing to 4.0000 with nothing real changed. The policy cycle’s own counts check out at 4, 6, 7, 5 and 4, with the first day carrying more items than the whole sale process and only one of its seven being income. And the actuarial department is asked to maximise a constant: bonus and profit sum to the surplus at every split, at a trade-off of -1.0000 at any scale (Ch 1, Ch 36)
  • built02. Financial management — a reconciling analysis of surplus is not a correct one, it is a consistent one. The summary tells the actuary to total the components so they match the actual surplus, and every ordering does, at 200.0000 on the illustrative year; but the expense component is -525.0000 if expenses are analysed first and -550.0000 if interest is, and the 25.0000 gap is exactly the expense deviation of 500.0000 times the interest deviation of 0.0500, assigned entirely to whichever factor goes second. Mortality escapes it because it enters the fund additively rather than as a rate applied to it, which gives a general test for which components are convention rather than measurement. The sign of a surplus identifies the basis, not the performance: a book exactly on its best estimate shows exactly zero on a realistic basis every year and a positive figure on a supervisory one, totalling 150.0000, exactly the margin held at the start. New business changes the sign of its own effect between bases, from 200.0000 to -400.0000, crossing zero at a reserve of 400.0000 which is the premium less the initial expense exactly, and regular premium business is the worse case for the frequency reason. And the summary’s claim that embedded value is a better remuneration measure is half right: the growing manager’s advantage is the extra policies times the excess of assumed profit over strain, vanishing at 300.0000, so surplus penalises real growth and embedded value rewards assumed growth. Expense apportionment is a cross-subsidy decision dressed as a measurement. One observation recorded against the summary (Ch 35, Ch 36)
  • planned03. Capital management 03-capital_management.html — new business strain, why profitable business consumes capital before it releases any, and the five factors that set how much (Ch 1)
  • planned04. Stakeholders and governance 04-stakeholders_and_governance.html — policyholders, shareholders, regulators, reinsurers, distributors, staff, and the conflicts between their aims (Ch 36)
  • planned05. Policy lifecycle 05-policy_lifecycle.html — design, pricing, marketing and sales, administration, underwriting, claims, monitoring, valuation; the map that says which module owns each stage (Ch 1, Ch 36)

The capital management lecture is the hinge. It frames why reserves and solvency capital matter without doing the arithmetic, which is deferred to risk measurement. This mirrors how the banking track separates capital management from the capital calculations.
