Life insurance, internal environment

Products

The catalogue, and the vocabulary the rest of the track runs on. Eight lectures covering F102 chapters 1 to 7. Every product is taught the same way: what the benefit is, what customer need it answers, which product basis it can be written on, which of the insurer's risks it concentrates, how much capital it consumes, and whether a group version exists. Once that grid is in your head, a product you have never seen becomes a set of answers to six familiar questions.

How this module is organised

The framework comes first, then the bases, then the products themselves. That ordering is deliberate and differs from the syllabus, which introduces the product bases at chapter 4, after two chapters of products that have already used the terms. Teaching without-profits, with-profits, unit-linked and index-linked up front means the assurance and annuity lectures can name a basis without a forward reference.

Group versions are pulled out of chapters 1, 5, 6 and 7 into a single closing lecture. The syllabus scatters them, but the considerations repeat almost verbatim across products (no individual underwriting, free cover limits, experience rating, concentration risk), so they are better learned once.

Framework

  • built01. Product framework — the summary gives the whole of life insurance finance in one line, C0+ = V0+ + e0+ - P0+, and the sign of it is not a property of the product: a 300 reserve with 600 of expense against a 1000 annual premium releases 100.0000 of capital, while raising the expense to 900 or the reserve to 700 consumes 200.0000 or 300.0000. Of the five named factors premium frequency dominates because it alone changes the sign, taking the identical policy from -100.0000 annual to +816.6667 monthly, and at 40.0000 of profit per policy that strain cannot be funded from the year’s own profit at any volume, so growth is limited by capital rather than demand. That inverts the banking case exactly, since a bank earns from day one and a life insurer pays its acquisition cost up front. The BIIFIL risks show no basis removes risk from the policyholder, who bears between three and five of the six under all four bases, with investment risk the only one that genuinely moves and lapse risk theirs under every basis. And the life cycle is not a partition: five of the nine named products serve more than one stage, while the stage with no assets has no products, which is information rather than an oversight
  • built02. Product bases — the summary makes three comparative claims about simple, compound and super-compound reversionary bonuses, and supplies example rates that cannot test them: 2 per cent simple reaches 140.0000 against 1 per cent compound’s 122.0190, so any path comparison confounds deferral with generosity. Equalise the terminal benefit at 150.000000 and all three claims hold exactly. Super-compound reaches 120.7107 at year ten against simple’s 125.0000, so it defers most; it delivers 0.4142 of its bonus in the first half against simple’s exactly 0.5000, so it weights late profits; and its guarantee is worth 214.1788 against 246.7509, a 13.2004 per cent capital saving on an identical outcome. The three are one claim, since a benefit not yet granted is a benefit not yet reserved for, which makes the bonus method a capital decision dressed as a distribution decision and reduces lecture 1’s strain directly. Full deferral to a terminal bonus takes the guarantee’s present value to zero, and four of the summary’s six consequences of deferring are insurer benefits, one a cost and one the resolution: smooth what you defer, because two of the four things a policyholder expects are neither guaranteed nor reserved for

The distinction that matters most in the exam is who bears investment risk. Without-profits puts it entirely on the insurer, unit-linked puts it almost entirely on the policyholder, and with-profits sits between them with the insurer holding the guarantee and the smoothing obligation. Almost every later question about reserves, capital, guarantees or bonus policy is really a question about that split.

Life products

  • built03. Assurances — the cheapest contract to buy is the most capital-hungry to write. Term assurance has the smallest reserve of four contracts at 50 and the largest strain per unit of premium at 1.2500, while a single-premium endowment has the largest reserve at 9000 and releases capital; holding reserve and expense fixed, the ratio falls from 1.2500 to -0.6250 as the premium rises, so cheapness is the mechanism and protection is the capital-intensive line while savings business finances itself. The summary defines mortality risk as the death benefit less the reserve, which makes it measurable: a term assurance has 99200.0000 of a 100000 benefit at risk against 12000.0000 for an endowment near maturity, a factor of 8.2667, which is the quantity reinsurance is actually bought on. Withdrawal risk is definable even for term assurance despite no surrender value, because the difference is zero less the asset share: an early withdrawal costs the insurer 3200.0000 and a late one costs the policyholder 2400.0000, so it changes sides partway through. The renewal option’s anti-selection has an exact price: if 80 per cent of healthy lives leave, the renewing pool’s mortality reaches 1.7347 times the original, and the loading required at outset equals the proportional deterioration exactly. The three risk lists nest at sizes four, five and seven, adding investment risk with a savings element and longevity risk with a survival benefit
  • built04. Annuities — for an annuity the reserving basis is not one input to the capital requirement, it is the capital requirement. The summary gives an immediate annuity essentially one capital note where term assurance got four, and the asymmetry is correct: a single point off the reserving rate moves the reserve by 7922.5313 on a 100000 premium, 0.0792 of the whole consideration, taking the strain from -10038.4796 at 0.0600 to 13866.2019 at 0.0300 and crossing zero between 0.0500 and 0.0400. The same proportional change is worth 0.0225 of premium on a term assurance, a factor of 3.8202, because the premium and the reserve are nearly the same number. That reverses lecture 1: single-premium business generates capital only to the extent the premium exceeds the reserve, so it is not automatically capital-generative. Anti-selection reverses too, since the healthy life is the dangerous buyer: a book sold only to healthy lives costs 12037.8061 more than priced and one sold only to impaired lives costs 27640.5208 less, which is why the impaired-life annuity exists. Priced separately the healthy life receives 7255.4818 and the impaired life 11497.6117 for the same money, a ratio of 1.5847, so higher benefits for a shorter life removes a subsidy rather than granting a concession, and it is the only remedy available because the insurer cannot decline. The guaranteed conversion terms are an option paying exactly zero above the strike and the fund times the gap below it, 40000.0000 at a market rate four points under a 0.1100 guarantee, and both drivers the summary names, falling rates and improving longevity, push it the same way (Ch 3)

The mathematics of these contracts is already covered in actuarial mathematics. What is new here is the commercial reading: a term assurance and an endowment can have identical death benefits and completely different risk profiles, because one is bought for protection and the other for savings, and the buyer's motive drives the anti-selection and the withdrawal behaviour.

Health and care products

  • built05. Income protection — the first thirteen weeks of cover are the worst value in the contract. The summary lists lower claim costs and lower admin costs as parallel reasons for a deferred period, and they differ by a factor of 3.4535: thirteen weeks removes 0.8300 of sickness episodes and only 0.2403 of the benefit cost, because durations are skewed and the cost lives in the tail while the paperwork lives in the mass. Price the tranches separately and the first thirteen weeks cost 1.3035 per rand of benefit against 1.0197 afterwards, and the ordering survives an admin fee of 1.0000 per claim, so it is the per-claim structure and not the size of the fee that makes short cover dear. The summary’s escalation prudence rule turns out to be the exact no-drift condition on the replacement ratio rather than a margin around one: a benefit escalating at 0.0500 against earnings at 0.0300 takes a 0.6000 ratio to full replacement in 26.5621 years, and across nine combinations the ratio drifts up if and only if escalation exceeds earnings, so the starting level decides when and never whether. Cost is convex in the ratio, rising 9.0000 times for a 1.8000 times benefit between 0.5000 and 0.9000, which makes a maximum ratio a solvency device. The four claim definitions are one dial: an initial period of 104 weeks captures 0.5549 of the available saving while leaving 0.9500 of claimants paid exactly as under own occupation, and the dial reaches both end stops exactly. And the guarantee loading is parameter uncertainty, so the morbidity-to-mortality ratio rises with scale from 2.4210 at one policy to 3.9999 at a million (Ch 5)
  • built06. Critical illness — one set of medical advances makes a term policy cheaper and a critical illness policy dearer. The summary names the rate of diagnosis first among the micro risks and says medical advances cause unexpected changes; the direction is the point, since progress that finds conditions earlier and lets people survive them cuts mortality and raises diagnosis at once, so a term assurance gets 0.2000 cheaper while a stand-alone CI policy gets 0.2500 dearer and the accelerated benefit moves only 0.0211, because bringing a claim forward is not the same as adding one. That also explains why narrowing the condition list buys a guarantee: the rare tranche is 0.0121 of claim cost, so dropping it saves variance rather than expected cost. The summary’s two statements about the list, that lengthening it costs minimally and that offices decline to, are both true of different costs: the rare tranche’s marginal cost per unit of risk transferred is 700000.0000 against the majors’ 101600.0000, a factor of 6.8898, and the summary’s own general conditions requiring clear definition and sufficient data are that fixed cost in words. The rider costs 22000.0000, exactly stand-alone 11400.0000 plus term 10600.0000; the survival period is worth 600.0000 on stand-alone and exactly 0.0000 on an accelerated benefit, so it is a definition of the insured event rather than an anti-fraud measure. And tiering’s uncommented premium clause doubles the effective rate on remaining cover by the third level, a ratio fixed by the tier shares alone (Ch 6)
  • built07. Long-term care — the unit-linked contract runs out of money at the age the claims arrive. The summary warns that rising risk charges may exhaust the fund and the cover will lapse, without saying when: the charge compounds at 0.1100 because incidence does and the fund at 0.0600, so the fund empties at age 86 and 0.3453 of expected claims fall at or after that, more than the whole of the first fifteen years of cover. At 80 the fund is still worth more than the premium paid, so nothing signals the failure. A better return defers it and cannot prevent it, and the middle guarantee, protection from age 90, is worth exactly nothing because the failure precedes the protection. Indemnity and cash are one transfer described from both sides: the cash contract saves 75000.0000, exactly the loading on the cost uncertainty, against an expected shortfall of 59841.3421, a ratio of 0.7979, so cash suits a buyer who can absorb the tail and nobody else. Care costs track wages rather than prices (Baumol’s cost disease), so a benefit indexed at 0.0300 against costs at 0.0500 covers 0.6807 after twenty years and halves after 36.0425, which is lecture 5’s escalation identity with the sign reversed and no remedy inside the contract. Informal care creates value only while the carer earns less than the formal cost, exactly. A benefit rising with deepening incapacity is 0.7019 self-funding but not free. And the mental impairment override caps definitional tightening: from one activity to five the activities route falls 0.9333 and the total only 0.8224, with the override’s share rising from 0.0132 to 0.6296. One observation recorded against the summary (Ch 7)

These three carry disproportionate exam weight relative to their premium volumes, because each one exposes a different failure of the standard mortality toolkit: income protection needs a multiple-state model with recovery, critical illness needs inception rates that shift as diagnosis improves, and long-term care needs a claim definition robust enough to survive thirty years of changing medical practice.

Group business

  • built08. Group products — a bigger scheme is barely safer and proportional reinsurance makes it no safer at all. The summary lists concentration risk only for group contracts and separately assigns catastrophe reinsurance only to group business; those are one result. With a shared event in the model the coefficient of variation stops falling with size, converging on 1.5803 rather than zero, so at ten thousand lives an independence assumption understates the risk by 7.1449 times, and the error grows with the very quantity thought to be protecting the insurer. A fifty per cent quota share leaves the ratio at 1.596058, exactly unchanged, because it scales mean and deviation together, while catastrophe cover takes it to 0.223383: proportional reinsurance is a capital tool and non-proportional a risk tool. The free cover limit is a break-even rather than a negotiating position, at 1000000.0000, being the underwriting cost divided by the extra mortality it uncovers, and the actively-at-work test pays from 25000.0000, a factor of 40.0000 lower, so the gap between the two break-evens is the free cover band. Voluntary take-up is anti-selection for the third time in the module, taking mortality from 0.003200 to 0.010000, and the loading is convex with the first tranche of non-joiners worth 0.0768 and the last 1.3281, which is why participation rules are floors. The continuation option is selected twice over, 1.3903 on why they left and a further 1.8507 on who exercises. And credibility explains the market’s shape: identical experience earns a 0.0238 discount at a hundred life-years and 0.4167 at ten thousand, so the largest schemes self-insure (Ch 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 30, 31)

Where this leads

Products is the reference module. Pricing takes each product and asks what assumptions it needs. Risk management takes the risk each product concentrates and asks how to underwrite or reinsure it. Risk measurement takes the benefit shape and asks what reserve it demands. Read the framework lecture before any of them.
