Investments track

External environment

Twenty lectures on everything the investor must react to but cannot control. Four forces shape the space a mandate operates in: the macroeconomy sets returns, the state sets the tax and policy backdrop, the regulator sets the boundaries of permitted action, and the index providers set the benchmark the manager will be judged against. The last of these is the least obvious and the most consequential, which is why indices get their own module.

How to use this

The four arcs below are independent; read whichever the exam question or the mandate demands. This module leans harder on already-built material than any other in the track, so most lectures open by naming a fundamentals or regulation lecture and then covering only what changes when you look at it as an investor rather than as an economist, a bank, or a lawyer. Status badges show what is built (built), planned, or a gap.

Course sequence

The macroeconomy

  • builtLecture 1: the macroeconomic environment and the trade cycle. A market leading the real economy by six months has completed 100.00 per cent of its move by the time a revised GDP print is reliable, so an economist must be right about the economy and an investor right about it earlier than the price; the remainder is positive only when the market's lead exceeds the six-month publication and revision lag, so cyclical strategies work best in the slowest-anticipating markets; a thirty-year project is 26.33 times as rate-sensitive as a one-year one, which is why utilities behave like bonds and joins two of the summary's own lists; sector rotation is an exactly symmetric bet, with the prize and penalty equal to 0.0e+00 as a parameter-free identity; and a firm with 800 of debt against 100 of operating profit loses 22.22 per cent on a one-point rise, which explains two of the summary's three financial sub-sectors and not the third
  • builtLecture 2: central banks and monetary policy. Forward guidance is diluted by exactly the ratio of the guided horizon to the maturity, so two years of guidance moves a thirty-year yield by 0.0667 per cent of a 100 basis point promise, one fifteenth, which makes the summary's appeal to expectation theory true and nearly empty at the long end; the fractional reserve system and a bank run are one ratio read two ways whose product is exactly one, since the multiplier is its reciprocal and the payable deposit fraction is the ratio itself; the reserve ratio is convex so equal steps have unequal effects, adding 250.0000 down two points and removing 166.6667 up two; an interest rate ceiling relocates lending rather than reducing it and is procyclical by construction; and quantitative easing is amplified at the long end by 12.5 times, the exact complement of guidance
  • builtLecture 3: interest rates and asset prices. An equity's duration is exactly one over r minus g, so a deep value stock runs 11.1111 years and a high grower 100.0000, a factor of 9.00 with nothing about their businesses entering the calculation, which makes the summary's growth and value styles duration buckets and a style rotation an interest rate trade; the price is exactly unchanged when the growth upgrade equals the rate rise, so a rate rise from inflation fear and one from a growth upgrade have opposite effects and the bond-equity correlation flips between regimes; the household channel is a property of the mortgage market, transmitting 4.00 per cent of household income when floating and nothing when fixed; property duration is one over the cap rate; and in a 40/50/10 portfolio the equity contributes 69.20 per cent of the rate sensitivity against the bonds' 22.15
  • builtLecture 4: sector classification. The summary's cyclical and defensive lists hold thirteen entries across ten sectors, so consumer goods, consumer services and telecommunications are classified as both and the scheme is ambiguous for 30.00 per cent of its own units, with its own "problems" item two lines earlier supplying the reason; cyclicality and duration are independent axes its single sort cannot hold, so a defensive utility at 33.3333 years is more rate-exposed than a cyclical bank at 12.5000 and rotating to defensives in a rate-driven downturn rotates into the damage; the sector explains exactly the intra-sector correlation of a stock's variance, so specialisation is least useful in the same three sectors; and a conglomerate at 40 per cent of a sector raises its measured beta 70.00 per cent to 0.6800 while still passing a 0.70 defensive threshold

The state

  • builtLecture 5: government objectives and the policy toolkit. The summary lists a stable exchange rate among its objectives and control of interest rates among its tools, and with free capital movement those are mutually exclusive: a hard peg forces the forward premium to exactly zero and the domestic rate to the foreign 4.00 per cent, so the government holds a tool it cannot use; it defines the fiscal deficit and omits the sustainability condition, which requires the primary balance to equal the growth shortfall times the debt ratio, exactly zero when the rate equals growth and 4.0000 per cent at a seven point shortfall, proportional to the debt ratio so eight times the debt needs eight times the surplus; issuance and quantitative easing move yields by equal magnitude in opposite directions, so net issuance is the quantity that matters; and three of the six objective pairs conflict directly, which makes the constraints forecastable where the objectives are not
  • builtLecture 6: taxation of investment returns and investor behaviour. The summary says the corporate system influences the dividend preference, and it decides its sign: a buy-back beats a dividend by 15.8400 under the classical system and loses by 0.9600 under imputation on identical rates, because the credit attaches to dividend income and not to a capital gain. The classical system takes 56.8000 per cent of a unit of profit and imputation 40.0000, exactly the investor’s own rate, so double taxation describes one system rather than company taxation. The two routes tie exactly when the gains rate equals the income tax rate. The interest deduction’s capitalised shield is exactly the corporate rate times the debt whatever the cost of debt, worth nothing without taxable profit, so identical debt costs two firms different amounts and the difference is the tax position rather than the credit. A regressive income tax inverts every wrapper conclusion of the internal environment. The minimum repurchase period defeats bed and breakfasting on a 1000 holding and leaves a 100 holding profitable, so the countermeasure is regressive in its incidence. Three of the summary’s six tax-specific factors are actionable, and a carried-forward loss is an option rather than an asset

The regulator

  • builtLecture 7: the principles of financial regulation. The summary calls information asymmetry the biggest cause of market failure, and Akerlof’s condition is exact: the average quality offered is half the price whatever the price is, so a market survives only if the buyer values the goods at more than twice the seller. The threshold is exactly 2, confirmed by search to twelve places and identical at every price, and below it the loss is the entire gain from trade rather than a fraction, largest in absolute terms just below the cliff, which is why a regulator cannot locate the failure it is correcting. Protecting consumers pays only if it raises the failure rate by less than 0.2542 percentage points on a loss of 100, so a doubling is fatal on a small exposure and tolerable on a large one, and the case for covering small balances is an asymmetry argument rather than a risk-aversion one. A three million compliance cost at a 0.75 per cent fee excludes every firm below four hundred million of assets: five million removes six of ten firms and leaves 92.2156 per cent of the assets served. The four types are a menu matched to the stakes rather than a ladder, with the total cost minimised interior to the summary’s own ordering, voluntary codes never winning, and prescriptive rules facing an interior optimum at ten with a residual gap that never closes
  • builtLecture 8: trust law and fiduciary duty. The summary asks the trustee to show fairness and separately to protect the fund, and under a pooled portfolio those are incompatible rather than merely awkward. With pensioners at duration 8 and actives at 20, setting the assets to the blended 15.2000 immunises the fund exactly at every rate move and immunises neither class: a one-point rise costs the pensioners 28.8000 and hands the actives exactly 28.8000, because the liability-weighted mismatches sum to zero by construction. The transfer is an identity, so it cannot be hedged at fund level where it is already netted to nothing, and no consolidated risk report can see it. Notional segregation immunises both classes and the total at once, and no single asset duration does, so the deed item naming classes of beneficiaries is what makes fairness achievable at all. Delegating to sixteen experts each four times as reliable leaves the trustee owning four times the errors, cancelling at exactly the ratio of the two error rates, and whether delegation helps turns on whether errors add or subdivide. A sound decision looks wrong 42.5634 per cent of the time over a quarter, and the professional trustee’s standard of care has an interior optimum, so the best available advice is the wrong criterion
  • builtLecture 9: market conduct and financial services provider regulation. The summary says avoid a conflict of interest rather than disclose one, and the arithmetic vindicates the verb: a disclosed conflict costs the client 7.2000 where the undisclosed one cost 5.0000, because the adviser moves further than the client discounts. Disclosure breaks even only above a discount of 58.3333 per cent, the ratio of the two product gaps, so removal is the only remedy whose success does not depend on the client’s sophistication. Separating front and back office cuts fraud by exactly one over the individual rate, 50.0000 times here, and geometrically again for each further pair of eyes. Under collusion the benefit retains exactly one less rho of its value, halving at a correlation of one half at every dishonesty rate, while the improvement factor collapses from 50.0000 to 1.9608 times, so the two measures of the control diverge sharply. Activism has an exact minimum stake of the cost divided by the uplift, 15.0000 per cent here and independent of company size, which no diversified fund reaches: a broad index fund faces a ratio of 30.0000 to one against acting and needs exactly thirty co-operating holders to reach the threshold, so the useful stewardship trigger is a stake rather than a governance test
  • builtLecture 10: corporate governance. The summary leaves solvent trading as a phrase, and it is the most precise item on its list: below a debt equal to the bad-state asset value the shareholders are exactly indifferent to a fair gamble, and above it they prefer one, with every unit they gain a unit the creditors lose. At a debt of 90 against a safe 100 they prefer a gamble destroying 30 per cent of the firm, so a board maximising shareholder value near insolvency acts against the company, and the summary’s fourth mark of good governance turns out to be a statement about creditors with a precise trigger. Non-executive independence and non-executive nomination are incompatible in steady state: a board converges to the nomination process’s bias whatever it started as, with a half-life set by turnover and a destination set only by the process, so moving nomination away from the chief executive raises retained independence sevenfold. Setting pay by peer comparison has no equilibrium, lifting the median 30.1693 per cent in five rounds with nobody paying above the upper quartile and terminating at the second-highest original value; targeting the median still ratchets, so the cause is the one-way adjustment and no percentile fixes it. Five of the five non-executive roles depend on somebody else, three on the monitored themselves, and detection is capped by the share of facts supplied rather than by diligence
  • builtLecture 11: ESG and responsible investment. An exclusion is free on the measure nobody uses and expensive on the one everybody uses: excluding one sector of ten costs 0.5013 per cent of the Sharpe ratio and creates 1.4907 per cent of tracking error, a ratio of 2.9740 to one, and the second is what trustees are judged against. Above an excluded weight of exactly two over the number of sectors the Sharpe ratio improves, because a cap-weighted market is itself the concentrated portfolio, and that threshold is identical at every volatility, correlation and sector count. The tracking error never turns negative, so no exclusion is free on both measures, and for a large sector the two disagree on sign rather than size. Divestment does reach the company: a fraction f divesting raises the required return by one over one less f, worth 26.3158 basis points at five per cent, and the divesting holder forgoes exactly what they impose, so every basis point charged is handed to whoever stays. It kills projects in a band exactly as wide as the rise, which reconciles an exact mechanism with small measured effects. A secondary purchase hands the company nothing, so at a two per cent primary share 980 of every 1000 of labelled flow acts only through the price, and the summary’s two clauses form an exhaustive dilemma in which ESG and fiduciary duty collapse into each other rather than conflicting
  • builtLecture 12: listing authorities and competition regulation. The summary buries the over-subscription process among nine prospectus items and it sets the issue price: under pro-rata allocation the uninformed receive 40 shares when the issue is good and 100 when it is bad, so the break-even price is 95.714286 and the discount 4.2857 per cent. A rule allocating the same number in both states removes the discount entirely, one filling the informed first prices the issue at the bad-state 90.0000, and the fixed retail tranche usually defended as fairness is the rule that lets the company sell dearest. The product definition clause has the sign reversed: narrowing eight firms to three raises the HHI from 1800.0000 to 3609.4675 and the merger’s effect from 1200.0000 to 2840.2367, so parties argue for breadth and regulators for narrowness. The import defence is true of the merger’s own contribution, which falls identically whatever the import structure because it depends only on the merging pair, and false of the level, which is U-shaped with two large importers because they become the concentration. Under a rate-of-return cap profit is exactly the allowed margin times the asset base with no turning point, reversing below the true cost of capital and vanishing precisely at it, while a price cap sets the consumer’s share at exactly one less the sharing factor
  • builtLecture 13: accounting standards and the investor. The summary gives IFRS three lines, all three correct and none with a magnitude, so this lecture supplies the three magnitudes. Asymmetry shows up as a spread, a spread is a round-trip cost amortised over the holding period, so halving a 2 per cent spread is worth 400 basis points a year to a quarterly holder and 5 to a twenty-year one, which inverts the usual argument for disclosure. The first jurisdiction to adopt a common standard delivers exactly zero comparability, 0.100000 before and after to fifteen places, because one standard with one user is a local standard; the marginal gain of the kth adopter is exactly 2(k-1) over the square of the count, so it rises linearly and the tenth delivers nine times the second. The zero first step survives unequal jurisdiction sizes exactly, making adoption a coordination problem rather than a cost-benefit one, which explains convergence stalling then accelerating. Transparency hides a reliability-timeliness trade with an interior optimum: a reviewed number at one month beats both an immediate estimate and an audited quarterly one, with audit worth waiting for below a decay rate of 1.426700. And the deferred tax asset promised in lectures 6 and 11 is priced: 112.0000 of face is worth 17.2421 at a 30 per cent probability seven years out, an 84.6053 per cent overstatement, and the probability is lowest exactly where the balance is largest, which is why Basel deducts it in full

The market

  • builtLecture 14: market participants and infrastructure. The summary gives an investment bank three objectives that order the issue price three different ways: 50.6544 for a 95 per cent chance of placement, 114.8345 for maximum expected proceeds and 140.0000 for maximum proceeds if it clears, so they are one objective and two constraints. The second is not independent either, since the underwriting premium rises with the very price the first wants, making all three the same dial. Intermediation stops paying at a wealth of exactly 80,000 divided by the holding period, so the households needing an intermediary on cost grounds are the small and frequently trading ones. But the real case is diversification: thirty stocks leaves only 2.0902 per cent of diversifiable risk and needs 60,000 of wealth, so a smaller investor cannot reach it at any price, and the four advantages bind in different wealth ranges. Stock lending and the exercise of voting rights are listed as parallel services and the first consumes the second: half a holding on loan yields 0.2000 per cent of the whole and costs half the votes, taking a 25 per cent holder lending 40 per cent to an effective 0.150000, exactly lecture 9’s intervention threshold. Three of the six participants pay and three are paid, and the three who pay are the supplier of capital, its user, and the supplier from abroad
