Investments, internal environment

Derivatives

Seven lectures on what the contracts are and how they trade. This module is deliberately descriptive: payoffs, contract terms, market mechanics, counterparty arrangements. Pricing lives next door in valuation, and what an institutional investor actually does with them lives in portfolio management. Splitting the three keeps each one honest.

How to use this

Lecture 1 sets up the exchange-traded versus OTC distinction that every later lecture refers back to, particularly the clearing house and margin machinery that explains why exchange-traded contracts carry no meaningful credit risk and OTC ones do. Read it first. Status badges show what is built (built), planned, or a gap.
