Investments track

Internal environment

Everything the investing institution controls. Six overview lectures establish what the institution is for and who decides, then seven modules work outward from the instruments it can hold, through how those instruments are priced, into how a portfolio is built, run, risk-controlled and finally judged. Seventy-nine lectures in total.

How to use this

The overview lectures come first and are short; they set the objectives and constraints that every later module answers to. After that the modules can be read in the order below or dipped into, though valuation assumes asset classes and derivatives, and portfolio management assumes portfolio construction. Status badges show what is built (built), planned, or a gap where the source summary is silent and the lecture draws on external academic material.

The institution and its purpose

  • builtLecture 1: the business model. Six links between household and asset take 1.9500 per cent a year and 52.08 per cent of a forty-year outcome, with the asset manager's fee only 30.77 per cent of the chain, so removing the fund-of-funds layer is 8.5 times as valuable as negotiating ten basis points; the loss is concave in the fee rather than convex, and its share FALLS as the gross return rises, which is why the cost debate intensified as yields fell; the summary's "need for intermediaries" names diversification where the operative constraint is the fixed dealing charge, since removing the minimum lets a 10,000 pot hold 500 names rather than 9; only the intermediary whose liability is defined as its assets needs no capital, and a pension fund's capital is the sponsor's covenant; a 15 per cent issue discount transfers five times a 3 per cent underwriting fee; and the custodian's tax reclaim is worth 150.00 per cent of the manager's whole fee
  • builtLecture 2: objectives, liabilities and constraints. Deferring the same capital gains tax at the same rate on the same gain is worth 33.63 per cent more wealth over forty years and beats realising annually at half the rate, so the summary's "minimise or defer" offers two options of very unequal difficulty; the summary states "not affected by what others do" as an objective and lists under-performing competitors as a risk whose suggested control is tracking them, and a liability-matched fund carries 16.8760 per cent tracking error against peers while a peer-tracking one carries none, so no strategy satisfies both; of the four liability parameters, three have matching instruments and longevity has none; a fund in surplus must hold a SHORTER asset duration, 11.4286 against a 12-year liability duration, because money durations must match; and the same bond is worth 4.36 per cent more to a tax-exempt holder than to a higher-rate individual

Who decides, and under what authority

  • builtLecture 3: governance, trustees and delegation. Sixteen managers each at an information ratio of 0.50 give the fund an aggregate 2.0000 and a three-year t-statistic of 3.4641 while every individual manager stays at 0.8660, so sixteen reappointment decisions at a five per cent error rate make a wrongful dismissal 55.99 per cent likely; that is the arithmetic of "delegate tasks but not responsibility", and the only remedy is fewer managers held longer since sixteen years are needed for one manager to reach significance; an ad valorem fee makes ten per cent asset growth worth exactly ten times one per cent of outperformance, and a performance fee converts that into an incentive to raise volatility because it pays 40.0000 on a gain and zero on a loss; and the summary's ESG objective and performance clauses contradict, since an exclusion costs exactly zero unless the excluded names were expected to outperform, and all six of its own examples are ordinary analysis of unpriced liabilities
  • builtLecture 4: mandates and the statement of investment principles. Six of Regulation 28's seven limits leave a conventional balanced fund double-digit headroom and the only one within five points is the 2.5 per cent other-excluded bucket, so the headline caps constrain nothing while the small buckets shape behaviour, and the same limits bind a growth fund on both headline caps and a matching fund on neither; the caps interact, since every point of offshore equity removes exactly a point of local equity capacity, so a rule limiting foreign exposure ends up limiting domestic equity and the feasible set is a polygon a mandate can breach without any clause looking wrong; a departure limit is either free or proportionally costly with the kink at the manager's intended bet size, so a single 2.0 per cent clause constrains the better manager and not the worse one; self-investment is exactly twice as damaging as an equal unrelated holding because the asset and the covenant fail together; and a 30 per cent forced government bond holding costs 12.7093 per cent over twenty years, disclosed nowhere

Structure and planning

  • builtLecture 5: vehicles and wrappers. One unit of income in one fund becomes 2.7970 in a direct holding and 5.9723 in a pension wrapper over twenty-five years, a gap of 3.0809 per cent a year available with certainty, so the wrapper decision is larger than the manager decision; there is no generally best wrapper because the ranking reverses with the marginal rate, and a zero-rate investor is actively harmed by a life policy; the crossover above which an internally taxed wrapper wins is exactly the internal rate plus the fee divided by the gross return, 35.0000 per cent here, matching a search to 4.4e-16 and independent of the horizon, which makes the decision asset-class dependent; the pension wrapper has the most fee headroom at 3.1905 points and a tax-free account's break-even fee for a non-taxpayer is zero; and the wrapper decides which prudential regime applies before any mandate is written
  • builtLecture 6: structure and financial planning. Earnings per share rises from 1.0000 to 1.2000 on a merger with no synergy whatever, purely because a firm rated at twenty times bought one rated at ten, and it is exactly unchanged at equal ratings and falls when the acquirer is rated lower, so the summary's seventh merger reason is an artefact whose available gain erodes from 20.00 to 8.25 per cent across four deals; a conglomerate's volatility equals a portfolio of its divisions to 0.000000e+00, so diversification is worth exactly zero and four of the summary's twelve reasons justify nothing; the summary states the principal-agent problem backwards, and management pay rises 7.50 per cent on every deal while the break-even synergy is exactly the premium; a 75-day cash conversion cycle needs 19.32 per cent of sales and the debtor lever beats the others by exactly sales over cost of goods; the inventory optimum equalises ordering and holding costs; and a covenant bars a positive-NPV project above 100.0000 of headroom
