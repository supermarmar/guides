Risk measurement, phase 2

Credit risk

The largest exposure on most bank balance sheets and the area where the modelling discipline has been pushed hardest. The track opens with a notation foundation file, then works through the two heavyweight frameworks (A-IRB regulatory capital and IFRS 9 impairments) as flat numbered sequences, and finishes with three adjacent topics. The wiki tree is the source of record; the lectures preserve the folder grouping (A-IRB, IFRS 9, application scoring, CCR, concentration) but flatten the lifecycle-stage subfolders inside each.

How this track is organised

The wiki under docs/wiki/application/banking/01_internal_environment/risk_measurement/credit_risk/ organises content by modelling lifecycle (introduction, data engineering, portfolio description, feature engineering, modelling, testing, ...). The lectures keep the framework-level grouping but flatten the lifecycle stages into a single numbered sequence inside each framework, so the reader follows one chain of files rather than navigating subfolders.

Status badges show what is built (built) or planned.
