Risk measurement, phase 2

Credit risk

The largest exposure on most bank balance sheets and the area where the modelling discipline has been pushed hardest. The track opens with a notation foundation file, then works through the two heavyweight frameworks (A-IRB regulatory capital and IFRS 9 impairments) as flat numbered sequences, and finishes with three adjacent topics. The wiki tree is the source of record; the lectures preserve the folder grouping (A-IRB, IFRS 9, application scoring, CCR, concentration) but flatten the lifecycle-stage subfolders inside each.

How this track is organised

The wiki under docs/wiki/application/banking/01_internal_environment/risk_measurement/credit_risk/ organises content by modelling lifecycle (introduction, data engineering, portfolio description, feature engineering, modelling, testing, ...). The lectures keep the framework-level grouping but flatten the lifecycle stages into a single numbered sequence inside each framework, so the reader follows one chain of files rather than navigating subfolders.

The track is complete: 55 lectures across the foundation, the two heavyweight frameworks and the three adjacent areas. Status badges show what is built (built) or planned.

PD component (wiki 04/pd, 05/pd, 06/pd + top-level 01_models.md, 02_probability_of_default.md)

Seven-lecture ML pipeline: concept → target & data → sampling → features → risk differentiation → risk quantification → testing. IRB-specific; IFRS 9 material deferred to the IFRS 9 track.

  • built09-pd_concept — what PD is, the three rating philosophies, the LRA anchor, the modelling-approach landscape
  • built10-pd_target_and_data — 12m worst-ever default flag, four feature families, data history requirement, low-default portfolios
  • built11-pd_sampling_and_representativeness — four-sample design (dev / cal / OOS / OOT), de-duplication, downsampling with weighting, PSI / chi-square / CDI diagnostics
  • built12-pd_feature_engineering — reduction (data quality, IV / Gini / R², correlation clustering, PCA, collinearity) and transformation (special values, missing, outliers, scaling, Tukey + Bulging Rule)
  • built13-pd_risk_differentiation — two-phase architecture, decision-tree segmentation, binary logistic regression, dual-model approach, five-criterion iterative refinement, directionality table
  • built14-pd_risk_quantification — joint calibration, 4-step grade construction (200 → 26), CRR 170(3) tests, LRA via PCA + F-test, overlap-bias check, monotonicity, MoC and floor
  • built15-pd_testing — 14-test battery: differentiation block (accuracy, Gini, PSI / CSI, parameter sensitivity, robustness, stress, benchmarking) and quantification block (HHI, homogeneity, heterogeneity, migration, calibration accuracy, monotonicity)

LGD component (wiki 04/lgd, 05/lgd)

Same seven-lecture ML pipeline. Wiki is sparse; the sampling / feature / testing lectures are supplemented from regulation (SS4/24 §13-16) and best ML practice.

  • built16-lgd_concept — LGD = 1 − PV(rec) / EAD, three-component mixture (zero/full/partial), four estimation methods, LGD1 / LGD2 split, downturn LGD and ELBE
  • built17-lgd_target_and_data — realised LGD with three component targets, pre / post-default features, SS4/24 discount-rate convention, MRP compounding of the 5/7-year history
  • built18-lgd_sampling_and_representativeness — default-vintage sampling, resolved-cohort cutoff (today − MRP), MRP truncation bias, cohort lag, three LGD-specific representativeness diagnostics
  • built19-lgd_feature_engineering — per-component branching (P(Zero), P(Full), partial recovery), LGD-specific feature families (collateral, workout-stage, recovery-market macro), tighter VIF and condition-index thresholds
  • built20-lgd_risk_differentiation — three-regression mixture (logistic for P(Zero) and P(Full | not Zero); Beta GLM for partial recovery), conditioning order, debt-sale and CoR adjustments, composite-LGD formula
  • built21-lgd_risk_quantification — calibration segments, LRA LGD, downturn LGD via max-over-candidates (LRA, SS4/24 §15.7 reference value, downturn period, add-on), ELBE for defaulted exposures, MoC
  • built22-lgd_testing — 16-test battery: differentiation block (MAE / RMSE / MPE, Spearman / Somers' D, component-level Gini, PSI / CSI, sensitivity, stress, benchmarking) and calibration block (LRA, reference-value compliance, downturn adequacy, ELBE convergence, segment monotonicity)

EAD component (wiki 04/ead, 05/ead, 06/ead)

Same seven-lecture ML pipeline. Key differences from PD: continuous target (EADF), GLM regression rather than logistic, fixed 12-month horizon sampling, R² / Lift for univariate screening.

  • built23-ead_concept — EAD = Drawn + CCF × Undrawn, five candidate targets (EAD / LEQ / EADF / CCF / UCF) with EADF as the standard, outstanding-amount definition, PiT / LRA / DT / Regulatory EADF architecture
  • built24-ead_target_and_data — EADF at the 12-month forward horizon, three feature families (at-observation, pre-observation trend, macro), look-back discipline, 5/7-year history without MRP compounding
  • built25-ead_sampling_and_representativeness — fixed-horizon 12-cohort construction (no de-duplication needed), 37 quarterly calibration snapshots, 80/20 OOS row-split, PSI / CSI representativeness battery
  • built26-ead_feature_engineering — R² / Lift screen, utilisation protected, triple-cap discipline (variables / realised / predicted at 1st / 99th percentile), link-space monotonising transformation
  • built27-ead_risk_differentiation — GLM (Gaussian + Identity standard; Gamma + Log / Beta + Logit alternatives), six-level segmentation cascade, direction-of-risk table, implicit limit-change treatment
  • built28-ead_risk_quantification — 10-15 grade construction on PiT EADF, 4-step LRA process (PCA + F-test), 2008-09 downturn anchor, max-of-paths regulatory rule (LRA + MoC_LRA vs DT + MoC_DT)
  • built29-ead_testing — 16-test battery: continuous-target accuracy and discrimination, PSI / CSI, max-of-paths binding check, implicit-limit-change diagnostic, calibration / monotonicity / migration

MoC, RWA, monitoring (wiki 07, 08, 09)

  • built30-moc — CRR 179(1)(f) anchor, EBA/GL/2017/16 three-category framework (A data, B changes, C estimation error), per-PD / LGD / EAD application, typical magnitudes, honest-vs-over-vs-under-MoC pathologies

IFRS 9 impairments

Same shape as A-IRB capital: flat numbered sequence inside ifrs9_impairments/, with the wiki's eight lifecycle folders unrolled.

Streamlined 12-lecture sequence focused on IFRS 9-specific differences from A-IRB. Each lecture cross-references the A-IRB analogue and highlights what is IFRS 9-specific (PiT not TTC, FLI scenarios, best-estimate not stressed, no MoC, no regulatory floor, monthly cadence, direct costs only for LGD, amortisation for EAD).

  • built01-context — ECL framework, three-stage model, FLI overlay, IRB-vs-IFRS 9 comparison across 10 dimensions
  • built02-definitions — six families of definitions: carrying amounts, credit-quality (SICR, impairment, DoD), lifecycle states (cure, write-off, PWOR), parameters (FiT PD, lifetime PD, EIR), interest mechanics per stage, portfolio metrics
  • built03-process_overview — eight-stage lifecycle, five parameter pipelines (PD / LGD / EAD / FLI / SICR), monthly cadence, audit interface, use-test applications, side-by-side comparison with A-IRB lifecycle
  • built04-data_engineering — shared A-IRB MRD spine, three IFRS 9-specific extensions (FLI macro feeds, contract-level granularity, monthly orchestration), §5.5.17 anchor, audit-vs-supervisor readiness
  • built05-pd_modelling — four candidate methodologies, the empirical term structure worked in full, and the reference period as the point-in-time dial
  • built06-lgd_modelling — the PWGD / LGW / LGC decomposition, where the sensitivity actually sits, the completion threshold and its censoring bound, the A-IRB gap decomposed, and the scenario-product error
  • built07-ead_modelling — the amortisation profile and prepayment overlay, the EIR channel measured, the 5.5.19 / 5.5.20 tension for revolvers, pipeline CCFs, and prepayment as an adverse-selection problem
  • built08-fli — the regression and standardisation, scenario asymmetry decomposed, Gauss-Hermite nodes versus round numbers, the lifetime factor, and OLS under-dispersion as a certainty rather than a risk
  • built09-sicr — threshold families and the relative-absolute crossover, estimation noise as the case for a floor, roll-rate quotas, the delinquency-based target, and the outcome period as the parameter that sets the stage 2 population
  • built10-ecl — the ECL sum and the four measurement bases, the staging cliff, the stage 3 interest switch, the provision matrix, the additive three-factor covariance error, and POCI
  • built11-testing — the three-tier battery, twelve stage transfer metrics, four factors for a staging definition, discrimination against calibration, and the cost of stage oscillation
  • built12-monitoring — the loss allowance reconciliation, four-way movement attribution, IAS 8 and ISA 540, the signal-to-noise asymmetry between the allowance and the charge, and the close of the track
