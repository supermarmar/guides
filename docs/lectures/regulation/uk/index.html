Regulation track

United Kingdom

Five lectures on UK bank regulation: the Bank of England as macroprudential authority, the PRA as prudential supervisor, the FCA as conduct regulator, the UK's post-Brexit rulebook (CRR near-final + SS3/24 + SS4/24 implementing Basel 3.1), and the MREL resolution regime. The UK was a Basel co-founder and an EU member until 2020; the architecture today is twin peaks (PRA prudential, FCA conduct) with the BoE as the parent of the PRA.
