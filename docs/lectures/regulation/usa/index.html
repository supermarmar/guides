Regulation track

United States

Four lectures on the US banking regulation architecture: the Federal Reserve System as monetary authority and bank holding-company supervisor, the OCC and FDIC as bank-charter and deposit-insurance authorities, the Dodd-Frank Act as the post-2008 rebuild, and US GAAP / CECL as the American accounting framework. The US is the only major jurisdiction with multiple federal bank supervisors operating in parallel, plus a state-level overlay, plus a separate consumer-protection regulator (CFPB). Complexity is the defining feature.
