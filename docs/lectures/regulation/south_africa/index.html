Regulation track

South Africa

Five lectures on South Africa's financial regulation: the SARB as central bank and parent of the Prudential Authority, the PA as prudential regulator, the FSCA as conduct regulator, the National Credit Act and Regulator, and the Banks Act 1990 as statutory backbone. South Africa moved to a Twin Peaks model in 2018 (FSR Act 2017): one peak for prudential safety inside SARB, one peak for market conduct outside it. Priority area for ASSA F107 candidates.
