Regulation track

International standard-setters

Twelve lectures on the three institutions that write the rulebook the whole banking world implements: the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (housed at the BIS), the Financial Stability Board, and the IFRS Foundation. The Basel narrative is the spine, traced from the 1988 accord through the post-crisis rebuild to the finalisation reforms that take effect in 2026 and 2027.
