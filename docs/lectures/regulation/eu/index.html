European Union

Four lectures on the EU implementation of Basel: two on the institutions (EBA, ECB), and two on the legislation (CRR, CRD). The EU is the largest single bloc implementing Basel directly into binding law, through a directly-applicable regulation (CRR) and a transposing directive (CRD). The same Basel III rule appears as a CRR article in Brussels, a national rule in each member state, and a supervisory expectation under the SSM.
