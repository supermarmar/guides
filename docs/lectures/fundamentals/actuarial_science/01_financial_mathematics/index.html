Course companion

Financial mathematics

A seven-lecture series rebuilt from the University of Pretoria Financial Mathematics summary (IFoA CB1 / CT1 territory). The mechanical foundations every later actuarial subject assumes: how to describe a cashflow, how to move money through time, how interest and discount rates relate, how inflation distorts both, and how level and increasing annuities compress repetitive cashflow patterns into one formula. The final lecture extends the source into project appraisal: NPV, IRR, payback, and the decision rules that turn a present value into a yes or no.

How to use this

Each lecture rebuilds one part of the source summary as a teaching session, with intuition first, every formula derived in full, and a worked example at the end. Lectures are sequenced so each depends on the previous: cashflows define the canvas, time-value-of-money paints on it, annuities are reusable shapes, and project appraisal is the decision layer on top. If you skip ahead, expect notation to be unfamiliar. Status badges show what is built (built) or planned.
