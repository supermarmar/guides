Economic models

Utility functions and risk aversion, the CAPM, and the standard short-rate term-structure models.

Section 9 of 10 Source: Curtin §9, IFoA orange book §9

9.1 Utility theory

Standard utility functions

Exponential
$U(w) = -e^{-a w}$, $a > 0$. Constant absolute risk aversion (CARA), $A(w) = a$.
Logarithmic
$U(w) = \log w$. Constant relative risk aversion (CRRA) of $1$.
Power (iso-elastic)
$U(w) = \dfrac{1}{\gamma}(w^{\gamma} - 1)$, $\gamma \leq 1$, $\gamma \neq 0$. CRRA of $1 - \gamma$.
Quadratic
$U(w) = w + d w^2$, $d < 0$. Increasing absolute risk aversion above the satiation wealth.

Measures of risk aversion

Absolute risk aversion (Arrow-Pratt)
$$A(w) = -\frac{U''(w)}{U'(w)}$$
Relative risk aversion
$R(w) = w \cdot A(w)$.

9.2 Capital Asset Pricing Model

Security market line
$$E_i - r = \beta_i (E_M - r), \qquad \beta_i = \frac{\text{cov}(R_i, R_M)}{\text{var}(R_M)}.$$ Expected excess return on asset $i$ is proportional to its beta with the market.
Capital market line (efficient portfolios only)
$$E_p - r = (E_M - r) \frac{\sigma_p}{\sigma_M}.$$ Sharpe ratio is the same as the market portfolio for any efficient combination.

9.3 Term-structure models

Spot and forward rates (zero-coupon bonds)

$P(\tau)$ is the time-zero price of a zero-coupon bond paying 1 at time $\tau$; $s(\tau)$ is the continuously-compounded spot rate; $f(\tau)$ is the instantaneous forward rate at time 0 for time $\tau$.

Spot rate
$P(\tau) = e^{-\tau s(\tau)} \iff s(\tau) = -\dfrac{1}{\tau} \log P(\tau)$.
Instantaneous forward rate
$P(\tau) = \exp\!\left(-\int_0^{\tau} f(s)\,ds\right) \iff f(\tau) = -\dfrac{d}{d\tau} \log P(\tau)$.

Vasicek model

Instantaneous forward rate
$$f(\tau) = e^{-\alpha \tau} R + (1 - e^{-\alpha \tau}) L + \frac{\beta}{\alpha} e^{-\alpha \tau}(1 - e^{-\alpha \tau})$$
Zero-coupon bond price
$$P(\tau) = \exp\!\left[-D(\tau) R - (\tau - D(\tau)) L - \frac{\beta}{2} D(\tau)^2\right], \qquad D(\tau) = \frac{1 - e^{-\alpha \tau}}{\alpha}.$$
SDE form
$dr_t = \alpha(L - r_t)\,dt + \sigma\,dB_t$. Mean-reverting Gaussian short rate. See also Black-Scholes section for the full family.
