Course companion

Risk modelling

An eight-lecture series rebuilt from the University of Pretoria course Reinsurance & Risk Models (WST322 / CS2 chapters 13 to 20). Pure actuarial machinery: loss distributions, compound risk models, reinsurance arithmetic, ruin theory, and Bayesian credibility. The maths is the same maths the credit and operational risk desks use; the actuarial framing is just the cleanest place to learn it.

How to use this

Each lecture rebuilds one source chapter as a teaching session, with intuition first, formulae fully derived where the source skipped steps, and a worked example at the end. Lectures are sequenced so each one depends on the previous; if you skip ahead, expect notation to be unfamiliar. Status badges show what is built (built) or planned.

Course sequence

  • builtRisk models I: the individual and collective models

Applications

Bayesian and credibility

  • builtCredibility theory: blending experience with the collateral
  • builtEmpirical Bayes credibility: estimating structural parameters from the data
