Survival models

Mortality laws, empirical survival estimators, Markov multi-state models, graduation tests, multiple decrements.

Section 5 of 10 Source: Curtin §5, IFoA orange book §5

5.1 Mortality laws

Survival probability from force of mortality
$${}_t p_x = \exp\!\left(-\int_0^t \mu_{x+s}\,ds\right)$$
Gompertz's law
$\mu_x = B c^x$, ${}_t p_x = g^{c^x (c^t - 1)}$ where $g = e^{-B / \log c}$.
Makeham's law
$\mu_x = A + B c^x$, ${}_t p_x = s^t g^{c^x (c^t - 1)}$ where $s = e^{-A}$.
Gompertz-Makeham $\text{GM}(r, s)$
$\mu_x = \text{poly}_1(t) + \exp[\text{poly}_2(t)]$, where $t$ is a linear function of $x$ and $\text{poly}_1, \text{poly}_2$ are polynomials of degree $r$ and $s$.

5.2 Empirical estimation

Greenwood's formula
Variance of the Kaplan-Meier estimator $\hat{F}(t) = 1 - \hat{S}(t)$: $$\widehat{\text{var}}\big[\hat{F}(t)\big] = \big[1 - \hat{F}(t)\big]^2 \sum_{t_j \leq t} \frac{d_j}{n_j (n_j - d_j)},$$ with $d_j$ deaths and $n_j$ at risk at time $t_j$.
Nelson-Aalen variance
For the integrated-hazard estimator $\hat{\Lambda}(t) = \sum_{t_j \leq t} d_j / n_j$, $$\widehat{\text{var}}\big[\hat{\Lambda}(t)\big] = \sum_{t_j \leq t} \frac{d_j (n_j - d_j)}{n_j^3}.$$

5.3 Mortality assumptions over a year of age

Uniform distribution of deaths
${}_t q_x = t \cdot q_x$ for integer $x$ and $0 \leq t \leq 1$ (UDD).
Constant force
${}_t p_x = (p_x)^t$, i.e. $\mu_{x+t}$ constant in $t \in [0, 1)$.
Balducci assumption
$1 - {}_{1-t} q_{x+t} = (1 - t) q_x$ for $0 \leq t \leq 1$.

5.4 General Markov multi-state model

Kolmogorov forward equation
For transition probability ${}_t p_x^{gh}$ from state $g$ to $h$ over time $t$, $$\frac{\partial}{\partial t} {}_t p_x^{gh} = \sum_{j \neq h}\!\left({}_t p_x^{gj} \mu_{x+t}^{jh} - {}_t p_x^{gh} \mu_{x+t}^{hj}\right).$$

5.5 Graduation tests

Grouping of signs test
With $n_1$ positive and $n_2$ negative deviations, and $G$ the observed number of positive runs, $$P(G = t) = \frac{\binom{n_1 - 1}{t - 1} \binom{n_2 + 1}{t}}{\binom{n_1 + n_2}{n_1}},$$ and approximately $$G \sim N\!\left(\frac{n_1 (n_2 + 1)}{n_1 + n_2}, \frac{(n_1 n_2)^2}{(n_1 + n_2)^3}\right).$$ Critical values tabulated in the IFoA / Curtin statistical tables for small $n_1, n_2$.
Serial correlation test
$$r_j \approx \frac{\dfrac{1}{m - j} \sum_{i=1}^{m-j} (z_i - \bar{z})(z_{i+j} - \bar{z})}{\dfrac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^{m} (z_i - \bar{z})^2},$$ with $r_j \sqrt{m} \sim N(0, 1)$ approximately under no serial correlation.
Variance adjustment factor (duplicate policies)
$$r_x = \frac{\sum_i i^2 \pi_i}{\sum_i i \pi_i},$$ where $\pi_i$ is the proportion of lives at age $x$ holding exactly $i$ policies.

5.6 Multiple decrement tables

Three independent decrements under UDD
For decrements $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ each uniform over $(x, x+1)$ in its single-decrement table, $$(aq)_x^{\alpha} = q_x^{\alpha}\!\left[1 - \tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(q_x^{\beta} + q_x^{\gamma}\right) + \tfrac{1}{3} q_x^{\beta} q_x^{\gamma}\right].$$ Analogous formulae hold for $(aq)_x^{\beta}$, $(aq)_x^{\gamma}$.
