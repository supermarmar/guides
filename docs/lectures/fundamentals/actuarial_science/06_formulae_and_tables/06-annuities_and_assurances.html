Annuities and assurances

Life-contingent expected values: moments, premium-reserve relationships, and Thiele's differential equation.

Section 6 of 10 Source: Curtin §6, IFoA orange book §6

6.1 Approximations for non-annual annuities

Whole-life, payable $m$thly in advance
$$\ddot{a}_x^{(m)} \approx \ddot{a}_x - \frac{m - 1}{2m}$$
Temporary, payable $m$thly in advance
$$\ddot{a}_{x:\overline{n}|}^{(m)} \approx \ddot{a}_{x:\overline{n}|} - \frac{m - 1}{2m}\!\left(1 - \frac{D_{x+n}}{D_x}\right)$$

UDD-based identities relating $\ddot{a}^{(m)}$, $\bar{a}$, $A^{(m)}$ and $\bar{A}$ are also standard and given in the orange book.

6.2 Moments of annuities and assurances

$K_x$ and $T_x$ are the curtate and complete future lifetimes of a life aged exactly $x$, with $K_x = \lfloor T_x \rfloor$.

Whole-life assurances

Mean, variance (year of death)
$E[v^{K_x + 1}] = A_x$, $\text{var}[v^{K_x + 1}] = {}^2 A_x - (A_x)^2$.
Mean, variance (moment of death)
$E[v^{T_x}] = \bar{A}_x$, $\text{var}[v^{T_x}] = {}^2 \bar{A}_x - (\bar{A}_x)^2$.

Analogous identities hold for endowment assurances (status $x:\overline{n}|$), pure endowments ($x: \frac{1}{\overline{n}|}$), term assurances ($\frac{1}{x}:\overline{n}|$) and deferred whole-life assurances ($_{m|} x$). The ${}^2$ prefix denotes the same expectation taken at interest rate $(1+i)^2 - 1$ (i.e. discounting by $v^2$ per year).

Whole-life annuities

Mean, variance (annual in advance)
$E[\ddot{a}_{\overline{K_x + 1}|}] = \ddot{a}_x$, $\text{var}[\ddot{a}_{\overline{K_x + 1}|}] = \dfrac{{}^2 A_x - (A_x)^2}{d^2}$.
Mean, variance (continuous)
$E[\bar{a}_{\overline{T_x}|}] = \bar{a}_x$, $\text{var}[\bar{a}_{\overline{T_x}|}] = \dfrac{{}^2 \bar{A}_x - (\bar{A}_x)^2}{\delta^2}$.

Analogous identities hold for temporary annuities (status $x:\overline{n}|$).

6.3 Premiums and reserves

Premium conversion relationships

Whole-life
$A_x = 1 - d \ddot{a}_x$ and $\bar{A}_x = 1 - \delta \bar{a}_x$.
Endowment
$A_{x:\overline{n}|} = 1 - d \ddot{a}_{x:\overline{n}|}$ and $\bar{A}_{x:\overline{n}|} = 1 - \delta \bar{a}_{x:\overline{n}|}$.

Net premium reserves

Whole-life (annual)
$${}_t V_x = 1 - \frac{\ddot{a}_{x+t}}{\ddot{a}_x}$$
Whole-life (continuous)
$${}_t \bar{V}_x = 1 - \frac{\bar{a}_{x+t}}{\bar{a}_x}$$

The same forms hold for endowment assurance policies with statuses $x:\overline{n}|$ and $x+t:\overline{n-t}|$.

Retrospective and prospective
Under net premium equivalence the reserve equals the expected present value of future benefits less future premiums (prospective), or accumulated past premiums less accumulated past benefits (retrospective). Both give the same answer.

6.4 Thiele's differential equation

Whole-life assurance, continuous
For a whole-life assurance on $(x)$ with continuous benefit $1$ payable at the moment of death, continuous premium rate $\bar{P}$: $$\frac{d}{dt} {}_t \bar{V}_x = \delta \cdot {}_t \bar{V}_x + \bar{P} - \mu_{x+t}\!\left(1 - {}_t \bar{V}_x\right).$$ Interpretation: reserve grows with interest plus premium income, less the expected death strain at age $x+t$.
Multiple-state generalisation
For reserve $V_i(t)$ in state $i$ at time $t$, with benefit $B_i(t)$ payable continuously while in state $i$, lump benefit $S_{ij}(t)$ payable on transition $i \to j$, and force of interest $\delta(t)$: $$\frac{d}{dt} V_i(t) = \delta(t) V_i(t) - B_i(t) - \sum_{j \neq i} \mu_x^{ij}(t)\!\left[S_{ij}(t) + V_j(t) - V_i(t)\right].$$
