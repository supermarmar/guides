Exam reference

Formulae and tables

A dense formula sheet for actuarial exams, organised by topic. This is a reference for last-minute revision and in-exam recall, not a teaching set. The lectures in the surrounding tracks (financial mathematics, actuarial mathematics, risk modelling) build the intuition behind each result.

How to use this

Each page below covers one Curtin orange book section. Formulas are stripped of teaching scaffolding: no banners, no lede paragraphs, no callouts. If a formula is unfamiliar, jump to the matching lecture for the derivation, then come back here to memorise the result. The international actuarial notation key sits at the bottom of this page.

Sources

The spine of this reference is the Curtin Student Actuarial Society's Summary of Actuarial Formulae and Tables (version 2, February 2019), itself an annotated extract of the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries orange book. A small number of supplementary results are pulled from the IFoA Formulae and Tables for Actuarial Examinations (1980) where they remain useful and on-syllabus. Obsolete IFoA material (finite differences, classical interpolation) and the numerical lookup tables (Normal, t, chi-square, F, mortality) are not reproduced here, as both source PDFs already serve those well.

International actuarial notation

The orange-book notation conventions used throughout sections 4 to 6. Greek letters and life-table symbols not listed here are defined in context at first use.

Interest functions

i, d
Effective annual rate of interest and rate of discount. Related by $d = i / (1+i)$ and $1 - d = v$.
v
Discount factor, $v = (1+i)^{-1}$.
$\delta$
Force of interest, $\delta = \ln(1+i)$, the continuously-compounded equivalent rate.
$i^{(m)}, d^{(m)}$
Nominal rates of interest and discount convertible $m$ times per year. $(1 + i^{(m)}/m)^m = 1+i$.

Annuities-certain

$a_{\overline{n}|}, \ddot{a}_{\overline{n}|}$
Present value of a level annuity-certain of 1 per period for $n$ periods, in arrears and in advance respectively.
$s_{\overline{n}|}, \ddot{s}_{\overline{n}|}$
Accumulated value at time $n$ of the same annuities.
$\bar{a}_{\overline{n}|}$
Present value of a continuously-payable annuity-certain at unit rate for $n$ periods.
$(Ia)_{\overline{n}|}, (Da)_{\overline{n}|}$
Increasing and decreasing annuities-certain in arrears.

Life-table functions

$\ell_x$
Expected number of lives alive at exact age $x$.
$d_x$
Expected number of deaths between ages $x$ and $x+1$, equal to $\ell_x - \ell_{x+1}$.
$q_x, p_x$
Probability that a life aged $x$ dies before age $x+1$, and probability of survival to $x+1$. $p_x = 1 - q_x$.
${}_t p_x, {}_t q_x$
Probability that a life aged $x$ survives, or dies, within $t$ years.
$\mu_x$
Force of mortality at exact age $x$.
$\stackrel{\circ}{e}_x, e_x$
Complete and curtate expectation of life from age $x$.

Life annuities and assurances

$a_x, \ddot{a}_x, \bar{a}_x$
Expected present value of a whole-life annuity of 1 per year on $(x)$, payable in arrears, in advance, and continuously respectively.
$a_{x:\overline{n}|}$
Temporary annuity, paid only while $(x)$ survives and for at most $n$ years.
$A_x, \bar{A}_x$
Expected present value of a unit whole-life assurance on $(x)$, paid at the end of year of death and at the moment of death respectively.
${}^2 A_x$
Same as $A_x$ but discounted at the rate giving $v^2$ each year. Used to compute $\text{var}[v^{K_x+1}] = {}^2 A_x - (A_x)^2$.
$A^1_{x:\overline{n}|}, A_{x:\overline{n}|}{}^1$
Term assurance (pays on death within $n$ years) and pure endowment (pays on survival to $x+n$).
$A_{x:\overline{n}|}$
Endowment assurance, equal to $A^1_{x:\overline{n}|} + A_{x:\overline{n}|}{}^1$.
$P_x, {}_t V_x$
Net premium and net premium reserve at duration $t$ for a whole-life policy on $(x)$.
$K_x, T_x$
Curtate and complete future lifetime of $(x)$. $K_x = \lfloor T_x \rfloor$.

Commutation functions (constant interest)

$D_x$
$v^x \ell_x$. The basic discounted survivor count.
$N_x, S_x$
$N_x = \sum_{t \geq 0} D_{x+t}$ and $S_x = \sum_{t \geq 0} N_{x+t}$. Used in $\ddot{a}_x = N_x / D_x$.
$C_x, M_x, R_x$
$C_x = v^{x+1} d_x$, $M_x = \sum C_{x+t}$, $R_x = \sum M_{x+t}$. Used in $A_x = M_x / D_x$.
