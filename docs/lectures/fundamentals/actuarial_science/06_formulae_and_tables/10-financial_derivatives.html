Financial derivatives

Forward and futures pricing, the binomial tree, Itô calculus, the Black-Scholes PDE and Garman-Kohlhagen formulae, and put-call parity. $q$ denotes the continuously-payable dividend rate throughout.

Section 10 of 10 Source: Curtin §10, IFoA orange book §10

10.1 Forward and futures pricing

Asset with fixed income of present value $I$
$$F = (S_0 - I) e^{r T}$$
Asset paying continuous dividend yield $q$
$$F = S_0 e^{(r - q) T}$$
Currency forward
$F = S_0 e^{(r - r_f) T}$, with $r_f$ the foreign risk-free rate.

10.2 Binomial pricing (tree model)

Risk-neutral probability of an up-step
$$p^* = \frac{e^{r \Delta t} - d}{u - d}$$
Calibration (Cox-Ross-Rubinstein)
$$u \approx e^{\sigma \sqrt{\Delta t} + q \Delta t}, \qquad d \approx e^{-\sigma \sqrt{\Delta t} + q \Delta t}.$$
Pricing recursion
$f_t = e^{-r \Delta t}\!\left[p^* f_{t + \Delta t}^{\text{up}} + (1 - p^*) f_{t + \Delta t}^{\text{down}}\right]$, applied recursively from expiry back to time zero.

10.3 Stochastic differential equations

Generalised Wiener process
$dx = a\,dt + b\,dz$, with $a, b$ constants and $dz$ the increment of a standard Brownian motion.
Itô process
$dx = a(x, t)\,dt + b(x, t)\,dz$.
Itô's lemma
For $G(x, t)$ with $x$ an Itô process, $$dG = \left(a \frac{\partial G}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial G}{\partial t} + \tfrac{1}{2} b^2 \frac{\partial^2 G}{\partial x^2}\right) dt + b \frac{\partial G}{\partial x}\,dz.$$

Short-rate models

Ho-Lee
$dr = \theta(t)\,dt + \sigma\,dz$.
Hull-White
$dr = [\theta(t) - a r]\,dt + \sigma\,dz$.
Vasicek
$dr = a(b - r)\,dt + \sigma\,dz$.
Cox-Ingersoll-Ross
$dr = a(b - r)\,dt + \sigma \sqrt{r}\,dz$.

10.4 Black-Scholes for European options

Geometric Brownian motion for stock price
$dS_t = S_t(\mu\,dt + \sigma\,dz)$.
Black-Scholes PDE
For derivative value $f(S_t, t)$ on a dividend-paying underlying: $$\frac{\partial f}{\partial t} + (r - q) S_t \frac{\partial f}{\partial S_t} + \tfrac{1}{2} \sigma^2 S_t^2 \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial S_t^2} = r f.$$
Garman-Kohlhagen call and put
$$c_t = S_t e^{-q (T - t)} \Phi(d_1) - K e^{-r (T - t)} \Phi(d_2)$$ $$p_t = K e^{-r (T - t)} \Phi(-d_2) - S_t e^{-q (T - t)} \Phi(-d_1)$$ with $$d_1 = \frac{\log(S_t/K) + (r - q + \tfrac{1}{2}\sigma^2)(T - t)}{\sigma \sqrt{T - t}}, \quad d_2 = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{T - t}.$$

10.5 Put-call parity

European options on dividend-paying underlying
$$c_t + K e^{-r (T - t)} = p_t + S_t e^{-q (T - t)}.$$
No-dividend special case
$c_t - p_t = S_t - K e^{-r (T - t)}$.
