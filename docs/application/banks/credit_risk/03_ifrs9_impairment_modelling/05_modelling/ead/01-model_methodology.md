# Model Methodology (EAD)

## "Pipeline" Accounts

For loans that have been agreed / committed to but are yet to start (known as off-balance sheet loans). This risk of not going onto the book needs to be factored in. In other words the likelihood of the account not being drawn down - enter CCF.

At some firms a CCF can be applied as a multiplier to get more accurate EADs for both IFRS9 and IRB. Essentially the CCF value drops as months-since-offered increase, so pipeline acounts with higher time months-since-offered are assigned proportionately lower CCFs. After 6 months usually the CCF reaches 0% with the intial starting values around 80%/90% with different multipliers per month e.g. 90, 70, 50, 30, 10, 0.01%.

Alternatively, a prudent but arbitrary number can be used as the CCF by looking at historical conversion rates. But its usually such a tiny component of ECL that it is not material.
