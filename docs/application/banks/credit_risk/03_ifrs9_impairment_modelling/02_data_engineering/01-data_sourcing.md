# Data Sourcing & Collection

IFRS9 states that "reasonable and supporatable" information should be used for impairment model development. That is information which is reasonable available at the reporitng date without undue cost or effort, including information about past eventsm current conditions and forecasts of future economic conditions. The information shall include:

- factors that are specific to the borrower
- general economic conditions and an assessment of both the current as well as the forecast direction of conditions at the reporting date

An entity may use various sources that may be both internal (entity-specific) and external:

- internal historical credit loss experience
- internal raitnsg and external ratings
- credit loss experience of other entities
