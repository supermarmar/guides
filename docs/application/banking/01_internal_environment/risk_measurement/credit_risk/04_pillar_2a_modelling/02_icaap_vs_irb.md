# ICAAP vs IRB

| Feature | Regulatory Capital (Pillar 1) | Economic Capital (ICAAP) |
| - | - | - |
| Primary Driver | "Standardized regulatory rules and minimum requirements (e.g. 8% of RWA)." | "Internal risk appetite, business strategy, and sophisticated risk modeling." |
| Risk Scope | "Focused on Credit, Market and Operational risks." |"Covers all material risks, including those not captured in Pillar 1, such as Pension or Concentration risk." |
| Methodology | Prescribed formulas to ensure consistency across the banking system. | "Internal techniques, stress testing (quantitative and qualitative), and risk correlations." |
| Goal |To meet minimum supervisory thresholds and ensure systemic stability. | To assess overall capital adequacy in relation to a bank's specific risk profile. |
