# Banks Act, 1990

<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/prudential-authority/pa-deposit-takers/banks-banks-act/2002/2591>

The regulations relating to banks under the Banks Act (South Africa) dictate the capital requirements. These regulations are set in line with the [[bis|Basel]] Accord. Including all amendments up to and including, the Banks Amendment Act, 2007

Its aim is to provide for the regulation and supervision of the business of **public companies taking deposits from the public**.
